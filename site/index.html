How to read it

The forecast combines a LightGBM model and a linear model trained on 25 years of ACA data (river gauges upstream, reservoirs and rain gauges). It uses only rain that has already fallen, so floods caused by rain in the lower basin can only be anticipated a few hours ahead.

Pre-alert: the upper bound of the 90 % interval exceeds the threshold. Alert: the forecast itself exceeds it. In 2025–2026, the pre-alert came before the flow crossed the threshold in 29 of 32 episodes (a median of 3.5 h earlier), and the alert in 21 (2 h earlier). Beyond a few hours the forecast peaks tend to be too low.

Model, validation and limitations: see the project repository.
